# Gul Bakes project overview

**Author:** Quratulain Shakeel  
**Report date:** 15 September 2026

## Purpose

Gul Bakes is an educational e-commerce and digital marketing project connecting a bakery storefront with brand content and customer communication.

## Documented work

- Shopify storefront organization, product listings, collections, navigation, cart, domain configuration, and theme customization.
- Canva branding and promotional content.
- Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, and Meta business tools.
- Brevo account setup, contact organization, domain authentication, email design, automation practice, and testing.
- Marketing frameworks including AIDA, STP, the marketing mix, customer journeys, SWOT, and KPI planning.
- AI-assisted ideation and content preparation with human review.

## Public documents

The public report and presentation remove private contact details, customer information, and account information from screenshots. Some original captions describe screenshots that are redacted in the public editions.

These documents describe a learning project. Proposed KPIs and future workflows do not establish measured business results. This repository is project documentation and does not contain a Shopify theme source export.
