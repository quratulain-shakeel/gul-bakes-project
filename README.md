# Gul Bakes

## Digital Marketing & E-commerce Practical Work Documentation

**Project author:** Quratulain Shakeel  
**Report date:** 15 September 2026  
**Context:** Educational e-commerce project developed through practical learning at Bano Qabil.

Gul Bakes brings together a Shopify bakery storefront, brand content, social media, and customer communication. This repository documents the project and its practical learning.

## Project scope

- Shopify storefront, products, collections, navigation, cart, checkout, and domain setup.
- Theme customization with Custom Liquid.
- Canva branding and creative assets.
- Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business setup.
- Meta Business Suite, business assets, datasets, Pixel, events, and advertising concepts.
- Brevo contacts, domain authentication, email campaigns, automation practice, and testing.
- AI-assisted ideation and content with human review and implementation.
- Marketing strategy using AIDA, STP, the 4Ps and 7Ps, customer journeys, funnels, SWOT, SMART objectives, and KPI planning.
